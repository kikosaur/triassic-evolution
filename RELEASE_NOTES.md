# Triassic Dinosaur Simulation - Release Notes v2.1.2
> **Thesis Project Interactive Simulation of Triassic Evolutionary Biology**

Welcome to the official release of **Triassic Dinosaur Simulation**. This update introduces the complete core gameplay loop, refined UI, and full educational systems.

## 🌟 Core Features

### 🧬 Multi-Dependency Evolution System
Experience a scientifically grounded evolution mechanic where species discovery isn't just about spending currency. To unlock new life, you must align three critical pillars:
- **Phylogenetic Lineage:** Evolve species from their direct ancestors (e.g., *Lagosuchus* evolved into *Eoraptor*).
- **Biological Traits:** Research essential adaptations like *Hollow Bones*, *Serrated Teeth*, or *Quadrupedalism* in the Tech Tree.
- **Habitat Suitability:** Terraform the land to support life. A *Riojasaurus* cannot thrive in a barren desert; you must cultivate **Vegetation** and **Critter** density to create a lush Jungle.

### 🕸️ Dynamic Ecosystem & Food Web
The world of Triassic Evolution is alive.
- **Trophic Levels:** Connect herbivores and carnivores in a delicate balance.
- **The Desperation Mechanic:** If carnivores run out of small critters to eat, they will turn on your precious herbivores to survive.
- **Habitat Visuals:** Watch the background shift dynamically from **Arid Desert** to **Blooming Oasis** and **Deep Jungle** as you improve the environment.

### 🏫 Museum & Education
- **Interactive Museum:** View detailed fossils and 3D-style representations of every species you unlock.
- **Scientific Accuracy:** All species, traits, and environmental shifts are based on real Triassic Period paleontology.
- **Badges & Achievements:** Earn recognition for mastering the ecosystem.

## 🎮 Gameplay

### Idle Strategy Loop
1.  **Gather DNA:** Tap the environment and dinosaurs to collect the building blocks of life.
2.  **Research & Evolve:** Spend DNA to unlock new nodes in the Phylogenetic Tree.
3.  **Manage Resources:** Balance your **DNA** (Growth) and **Fossils** (Premium/Prestige) currencies.
4.  **Maintain Balance:** Keep an eye on the "Hunger" and "Population" stats. An unbalanced ecosystem halts production!

### Controls
- **Tap Screen:** Collects active DNA.
- **Tap Dinosaurs:** Collects bonus DNA/Resources.
- **UI Navigation:** Access Shop, Research, Museum, and Settings via the main HUD.

## 🛡️ Ethical Considerations & Privacy

As an educational thesis project, we prioritize integrity and user safety.

### 1. Educational Integrity
- **Scientific Basis:** While simplified for gameplay, all evolutionary relationships and biological classification data are derived from reputable paleontological sources.
- **No Misinformation:** We strive to present facts accurately. Creative liberties (like time warping) are clearly marked as gameplay mechanics.

### 2. User Privacy & Data
- **Minimal Data Collection:** We only store game state data (save files) to allow for cloud saves and cross-device play.
- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not collect names, physical addresses, or phone numbers.
- **Ad-Free Experience:** This project is for educational defense purposes and contains **zero advertisements** or predatory microtransactions.

### 3. Accessibility
- **Visual Clarity:** The UI uses high-contrast colors and readable fonts (Inter/Roboto) to ensure text is legible.
- **No Forced connectivity:** The game is fully playable offline.
